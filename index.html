Step-by-Step Setup

How to Download and Install Unifly

Follow these straightforward steps to set up Unifly Shop Management on your phone or computer.

1

Choose Device & Download

Select your Android phone or retail tablet. Tap the official Download for Android button to begin downloading the APK installation file.

  • Tap "Download for Android"
  • Wait for the APK download to finish completely
2

Open Package & Grant Permission

Open the downloaded APK from your phone's notification bar or Downloads folder.

  • Open the downloaded APK file
  • If Android requests permission, allow only for your trusted browser/file manager
  • Review the installation prompt and tap "Install"
3

Launch & Sign In

Once installation finishes, launch Unifly from your app drawer.

  • Open Unifly Shop Management
  • Sign in or register your shop seller account
  • Start billing and managing stock right away
1

Download Official Setup

Click Download for Windows to retrieve the official installer setup (Unifly-Setup.exe) for Windows 10 or 11.

  • Click "Download for Windows (.exe Setup)"
  • Save Unifly-Setup.exe to your PC
2

Run Setup Wizard

Double-click Unifly-Setup.exe to start the installation wizard. Choose your preferred installation path and desktop shortcut options.

  • Run Unifly-Setup.exe
  • If SmartScreen appears, click 'More info' → 'Run anyway'
  • Enable Desktop icon for instant access
3

Launch Desktop App

Run shop_management_app.exe or launch from your desktop shortcut.

  • Launch Unifly from installed folder or shortcut
  • Sign in or register your shop seller account
  • Connect printers and start retail counter billing
